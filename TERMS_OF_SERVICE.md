# Translator Terms

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This is a copy of [https://montezproductions.lt/translator/terms](https://montezproductions.lt/translator/terms). If the two differ, the version on the website applies.

These terms apply to Translator, a Discord bot made by Montez Productions (the “Service”). They add to our general [Terms of Service](https://montezproductions.lt/terms), which also apply. If the two differ, these Translator Terms apply to the Service.

## 1. The Service

Translator translates Discord messages on request and shows the translation privately, only to the person who asked for it.

- You can translate a message with the **A → 文** button under it, or from the message menu (*Apps → Translate*). The message menu also works in direct messages.
- You choose your target language once with `/language` and can change it at any time the same way.
- The bot’s own messages are shown in your Discord language where available.
- Code blocks, inline code, links, mentions, custom emoji, timestamps and spoilers are kept as written.

## 2. Server administrators

Server administrators decide whether translate buttons appear under messages, for the whole server (`/translate_button`) or for single channels (`/translate_button_channel`). They are responsible for deciding whether this suits their server. Administrators can remove the bot from their server at any time.

## 3. Free allowance and paid packs

- Every user gets a free monthly character allowance, as shown on the [pricing page](https://montezproductions.lt/translator#pricing). It resets each calendar month (UTC). Unused free characters do not carry over.
- You can buy one-time character packs. Current packs and prices are listed on the [pricing page](https://montezproductions.lt/translator#pricing).
- A pack is valid for 30 days from the moment of purchase. Characters not used within 30 days expire.
- Personal packs belong to the Discord account that bought them and cannot be transferred. Server packs apply to one Discord server and are shared by its members.
- Characters are counted on the original text that you translate. A translation that fails is not counted.

## 4. Translation quality

Translations are produced automatically by machine translation and may be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed or unavailable. Check a translation before you rely on it, for example before you warn, mute or ban someone because of what a translated message seems to say, or before any legal, medical, financial or safety decision.

## 5. Changes to the Service

We may add, change or remove features and supported languages. Discord’s own availability and rules also affect the Service.

## 6. Privacy

How the bot handles data is explained in the [Translator Privacy Notice](https://montezproductions.lt/translator/privacy).
